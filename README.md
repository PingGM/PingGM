## ✨ What you can do

| | Feature | What it does | Try it |
|:-:|---|---|:-:|
| ☀️ | **Daily on-chain GM** | Send one GM per network per day (resets at 00:00 UTC). The PingGM contract records it on the chain you pick, building a public, verifiable activity record and a GM streak. | [**Send GM →**](https://pinggm.com/) |
| 📜 | **Smart contract deploy** | One wallet transaction deploys PingGM's Proof of Deployment contract on any supported EVM chain, giving you an on-chain deployment record. | [**Deploy →**](https://pinggm.com/deploy) |
| 🪙 | **Base B20 token launcher** | Create a B20 Asset or Stablecoin on Base Mainnet or Base Sepolia through Base's canonical B20 factory. No PingGM launch fee; you only pay gas. | [**Launch →**](https://pinggm.com/launch-base-b20-token) |
| 🤝 | **Referral rewards** | Share your link and earn recurring rewards (currently **15%**, set in the smart contract) on qualifying GM and deploy fees, paid instantly in the same gas coin. | [**Refer →**](https://pinggm.com/referral) |

## 🚀 How it works

1. **Connect** your own wallet (browser or any WalletConnect mobile wallet).
2. **Pick a network**, mainnet or testnet.
3. **Approve** the transaction in your wallet; the fee and gas are shown before you sign.
4. **Verify** it on the network's block explorer.

## 💸 Fees

Each GM and deployment costs a small **PingGM fee**, read from the contract on the selected chain, **plus network gas**. Both are paid in the chain's native coin and shown in your wallet before you approve. Testnets only need free faucet tokens.

## 🔒 Safety

- **Non-custodial:** you sign every transaction yourself. PingGM never holds your funds and never asks for your seed phrase or private key.
- Blockchain transactions are **public and irreversible**, and you pay gas even if a transaction fails.
