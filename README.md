PingGM ☀️

PingGM is a non-custodial, multi-chain Web3 app for sending a daily on-chain GM, deploying a smart contract, and launching Base B20 tokens. You sign every transaction from your own wallet. PingGM never holds your keys or funds.

🌐 Website: pinggm.com

What you can do on PingGM
Send a daily GM on-chain: one GM per network per UTC day (resets at 00:00 UTC). Each GM is a signed transaction recorded by the PingGM contract on the chain you pick, building a public, verifiable activity record and a GM streak. → Send your daily GM
Deploy a smart contract on any supported EVM chain: one wallet transaction deploys PingGM's Proof of Deployment contract, so you get an on-chain deployment record on that network. → Deploy a smart contract
Launch a Base B20 token: create a B20 Asset or Stablecoin on Base Mainnet or Base Sepolia through Base's canonical B20 factory. PingGM charges no launch fee; you pay network gas. → Launch a B20 token on Base
Earn referral rewards: share your link and earn recurring rewards (currently 15%, set in the smart contract) on qualifying GM and deployment fees, paid in the same network gas coin. → Referral program

Fees

Each GM and deployment costs a small PingGM fee, read from the contract on the selected chain, plus that network's gas. Both are paid in the chain's native coin and shown in your wallet before you approve. Testnets only need free faucet tokens.

Safety

Non-custodial: PingGM never asks for your seed phrase or private key.
Blockchain transactions are public and irreversible; you pay gas even if a transaction fails.
